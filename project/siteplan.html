Explore Uganda

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Explore Uganda

Explore Uganda is a website designed to introduce visitors to the beautiful landscapes, wildlife, culture, tourist attractions, and unique travel experiences that Uganda has

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Explore Uganda is to provide visitors with useful information about selected destinations in Uganda. The website will present destination descriptions, locations, activities, photographs, and categories.

Visitors will also be able to filter destinations using JavaScript and submit destination suggestions through an online form.

3. Scenarios

  • Which places in Uganda can I visit if I am interested in wildlife and nature?
  • Where can I find information about lakes and scenic destinations in Uganda?
  • How can I suggest a destination to be added to the website?

4. Color Schema

Dark Green #174A2A
Gold #D9A441
Cream #F7F3E8

Dark green will be used for the header, navigation, headings, and footer. Gold will be used for buttons, highlights, and accents. Cream will be used as the primary background color.

5. Typography

Montserrat

Montserrat will be used for headings and important titles.

Roboto

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, navigation, forms, and general content.
